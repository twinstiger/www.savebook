Help & FAQ

Quick start

  1. Paste any article URL into the home page input.
  2. Choose PDF, EPUB, TXT, or MD.
  3. Click Convert and wait a few seconds.
  4. Download the file, or send it straight to your Kindle.

Setting up Kindle push

  1. Find your Kindle email address on Amazon's Manage Your Content and Devices page.
  2. Add noreply@savebook.net to your Approved Personal Document E-mail List.
  3. After converting, click Send to Kindle and enter your Kindle email.
  4. Your file appears in your Kindle library within a few minutes.

Why does some content not extract cleanly?

SaveBook uses Mozilla Readability to detect the main article. Some pages (login walls, JavaScript-heavy apps, PDF-only sources) don't have extractable text. Try opening the page in a normal browser first and copy the link from the address bar.

Common errors

HTTP 403 / 401
The source page is paywalled or behind a login.
Conversion timeout
The page is very large. Try toggling off Keep images in Advanced options.
Unsupported content type
The URL points to a binary file — there's nothing to convert.
Kindle send fails
Check that you used your Kindle email (not your Amazon login email).
