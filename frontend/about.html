About

What is SaveBook?

SaveBook converts any webpage into a clean PDF or EPUB file for offline reading. No signup, no ads, no tracking — just paste a link and get your e-book in seconds.

How it works

We use Mozilla's Readability algorithm (the same engine behind Firefox's Reader View) to extract the main article content from any page, then generate a beautifully formatted e-book using pure JavaScript running on Cloudflare's edge network.

Privacy

We don't require an account. Conversion records are stored locally in your browser and expire after 7 days. Files are processed and stored temporarily in Cloudflare R2 before automatic deletion.
