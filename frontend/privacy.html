Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. Conversion records are stored locally in your browser and are not sent to our servers beyond what's needed to deliver the file.

How we use data

Uploaded URLs are processed only to generate the requested file. The file is delivered to you and stored temporarily in Cloudflare R2 for 7 days before automatic deletion.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. A session ID is stored in localStorage to associate your records with this browser.

Third parties

Files are processed using Cloudflare Workers and stored in Cloudflare R2. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
