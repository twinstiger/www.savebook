🎯 Speed Bonus Boards — All Locations & Tips

Speed Bonus Boards are scattered across the Japan map and are essential for leveling up your driver rank quickly. Breaking a board awards Speed Skill points and credits. There are three types: Speed Skill, Skill Attack, and Danger Sign. Use a high-speed car (300+ mph recommended) to chain boards on highways.

🗼 Tokyo Metropolitan Area

  • Shibuya Crossing Billboard — Central Tokyo, full speed from east approach. Skill point: ★★★★
  • Ginza Neon Sign — Ginza district, 150+mph required. Skill point: ★★★
  • Tokyo Bay Bridge North Approach — Long straight, 200+mph optimal. Skill point: ★★★★★
  • Akihabara Underground Pass — Tight entry, brake to 80 then full throttle. Skill point: ★★★
  • Odaiba Waterfront Straight — Waterfront highway, flat surface ideal for max speed. Skill point: ★★★★
  • Tokyo Ring Road East — Sign Jump — Ramp before board, air time counts as bonus. Skill point: ★★★★

🏔️ Hakone / Kanagawa Mountain Region

  • Hakone Turnpike Toll Booth Jump — Classic touge road, board at the hairpin exit. Skill point: ★★★★

  • Gotemba Trail Sign — Near Mt. Fuji base, steep downhill approach. Skill point: ★★★★★
  • Sengokuhara Highland Billboard — Wide road, easy entry and exit. Skill point: ★★★
  • Yokosuka Highway North Sign — Coastal highway, crosswind may affect speed. Skill point: ★★★

🚢 Yokohama Port Area

  • Minato Mirai Landmark Jump — Tall building approach, need early brake for ramp. Skill point: ★★★★

🏯 Osaka / Kyoto Cultural District

⚡ Pro Tips for Bonus Board Chaining

  • Use a car with 350+ mph top speed and good acceleration for board chains
  • Plan routes between multiple boards to maintain Skill Combo multiplier
  • Turn OFF traction control for more aggressive sliding initiation
  • Watch for boards hidden under highway overpasses — approach from correct direction
  • Night runs have less traffic but reduced visibility — balance risk vs reward

🏯 Hidden Japanese Landmarks — All Locations & Descriptions

Landmarks are scenic viewpoints and cultural markers scattered across the FH6 Japan map. Visiting landmarks contributes to the "Full Explorer" achievement and provides bonus credits and reputation points. Each landmark has a unique history or cultural significance.

Tokyo Metropolitan Landmarks

  • Tokyo Tower — Red and white latticed communications tower, built 1958. Best viewed at sunset when illumination begins. Location: Minato-ku, near Shiba Park.
  • Shibuya Crossing — World's busiest pedestrian intersection. Experience the organized chaos of 3,000 people crossing simultaneously.
  • Senso-ji Temple Gate — Asakusa district's oldest temple, main gate with vermilion columns and golden lantern.
  • Tsukiji Outer Market — Historic fish market area, fresh sushi and street food paradise.
  • Akihabara Neon Alley — Retro electronic district with massive LED signage and arcade glow.
  • Odaiba Gundam Statue — Full-scale Gundam statue at Diver City mall, moves hourly.
  • Tokyo Skytree — World's tallest tower at 634m, panoramic city views from observation deck.

Kyoto / Nara Historical Landmarks

  • Fushimi Inari Torii Gates — 10,000 vermilion torii gates winding up the mountain. Full run takes 2-3 hours. Best at dawn to avoid crowds.
  • Kinkaku-ji Golden Pavilion — Zen temple with gold leaf exterior reflected in mirror pond. UNESCO World Heritage.
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove — Towering bamboo forest creating natural cathedral-like corridor. Audio recording captures the distinctive rustling sound.
  • Nijo Castle Fortified Palace — Feudal lord residence with "nightingale floors" that chirp when walked on.
  • Todai-ji Daibutsu Hall — Nara — World's largest bronze Buddha statue at 15m tall, housed in world's largest wooden building.
  • Kasuga Grand Shrine — Nara — Ancient Shinto shrine with thousands of stone lanterns lining the approach.
  • Gion District Machiya — Traditional wooden merchant townhouses, geisha sightings possible in early evening.

Mt. Fuji / Hakone Scenic Landmarks

  • Chureito Pagoda — Five-story pagoda with Mt. Fuji backdrop. Cherry blossom season (April) or autumn foliage (November) provides ultimate photo opportunity.
  • Lake Kawaguchiko North Shore — Classic view of Mt. Fuji reflected in still water. Various art museums and hot springs nearby.
  • Fuji Fifth Station — Highest accessible point by road, marked trailhead for mountain climbers. Clouds often cover summit — arrive before 8AM.
  • Hakone Ropeway — Cable car across volcanic Owakudani valley, views of Mt. Fuji and sulfur vents below.
  • Lake Ashi Piracy Ship — Replica pirate ship cruising the volcanic lake with Mt. Fuji backdrop.

Hokkaido Natural Landmarks

  • Shiretoko Five Lakes — Remote wilderness area, pristine mountain lakes. Bear country — carry bear bell.
  • Noboribetsu Hell Valley — Volcanic steam vents and sulfuric smell, natural onsen hot springs. Drive through early morning for best experience.
  • Sapporo Clock Tower — Historic colonial-style building, symbol of Sapporo. Surrounded by modern city center.
  • Odori Park Sapporo TV Tower — Viewing platform for annual Snow Festival (February) when massive ice sculptures fill the park.
  • Cape Noba Cape — Northernmost point of Hokkaido, dramatic cliffs and lighthouse.
  • Takushino Flower Field — Expansive lavender and sunflower fields, peak season July-August.

Osaka / Kobe Landmark Highlights

  • Osaka Castle Tower — Reconstructed 16th century castle, museum inside with panoramic city views from top floor.
  • Umeda Sky Building — Floating garden observatory connecting two tower tops with glass elevators.
  • Kobe Port Tower — Red-colored tension-wire observation tower, shaped like a Japanese wind chime.
  • Chinatown Bund — Japan's largest Chinatown with authentic food stalls and neon signs.
  • Tennoji Park Keitakuen — Traditional Japanese garden with pond and tea house, tranquil escape from city.

🌸 Seasonal Limited Collectibles — All Seasonal Items

FH6 features dynamic seasonal collectibles that change with the in-game seasons. Each season (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) has unique collectibles that must be gathered before the season changes. Seasons cycle every 4-6 weeks in real time.

🌸 Spring Collectibles (March — May)

  • Sakura Petal Markers — 50 pink painted markers along scenic routes representing cherry blossoms. Found near parks and temple areas. Reward: 5,000 credits + Sakura Wings cosmetic item.
  • Spring Lantern Path — Traditional paper lanterns hung along canal paths in Kyoto and Nara. Reward: Lantern Glow headlight effect.
  • Matsuri Float Ornaments — Small festival float decorations hidden in village squares. Reward: Spring Festival emblem for player profile.
  • Hanami Picnic Sets — Cherry blossom viewing picnic spots with collectible baskets. Reward: Sakura carpet vinyl for cars.

☀️ Summer Collectibles (June — August)

  • Beach Volleyball Posts — 30 marked volleyball nets along Okinawa coastline. Reward: Beach party invitation to exclusive summer festival.
  • Sea Turtle Nest Signs — Conservation awareness signs at turtle nesting beaches. Reward: Sea Turtle vinyl wrap.
  • Summer Firework Points — Designated viewing spots for summer firework festivals (tanabata). Reward: Sparkle tire smoke effect.
  • Matsuri Food Stalls — Summer festival food stands with collectible dishes. Reward: Food vendor cabin customization item.
  • Marine Flag Markers — Racing flags at coastal beach areas. Reward: Ocean Wave body kit.
  • Ice Cream Vendor Locations — Find all 20 mobile ice cream vendors across Japan. Reward: Freezie horn sound effect.

🍁 Autumn Collectibles (September — November)

  • Autumn Maple Markers — Red and orange painted markers along mountain scenic drives. Found on Hakone and Izu Peninsula routes. Reward: Maple Leaf antenna topper.
  • Tsukimi Moon Viewing Set — Traditional moon viewing decorations in rural areas. Reward: Full moon headlight pattern.
  • Harvest Festival Ornaments — Small decorations at farm areas and rural villages. Reward: Harvest Moon cosmetic bundle.
  • Autumn Leaves Photo Points — Marked photography spots for autumn foliage. Reward: Fall Colors vinyl pack.
  • Mushroom Location Signs — Hidden in forested areas, identify edible mushroom varieties. Reward: Forest Green paint color.

❄️ Winter Collectibles (December — February)

  • Snow Sculpture Markers — 40 blue markers in snow-covered areas for Sapporo Snow Festival areas. Reward: Ice Crystal wheel rims.
  • Christmas Lights Trails — Festive light installations along city streets and resort areas. Reward: Christmas light underglow kit.
  • New Year Bell Locations — Temple bell locations for traditional New Year visit (joyakō). Reward: Temple Bell horn sound.
  • Ice Fishing Spot Signs — Marked ice fishing locations on frozen lakes in Hokkaido. Reward: Frozen Lake driving skill bonus.
  • Onsen Steam Vents — Collectible markers at natural hot spring steam locations. Reward: Steam cloud exhaust effect.
  • Winter Wildlife Tracks — Animal track markers in snow (deer, fox, rabbit). Reward: Snow Tires for winter driving.
