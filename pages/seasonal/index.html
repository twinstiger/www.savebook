How Seasons Work

Each FH6 season lasts 6 weeks. The Japan map transitions through Summer, Autumn, Winter and Spring — each bringing different weather conditions, drivatars and exclusive content.

Forzathon Shop

The Forzathon Shop rotates weekly with exclusive items only purchasable with Forzathon Points (FP). Earn FP by completing Daily Challenges, Seasonal Events and Festival Playlist activities.

  • Earning FP: ~500-1000 FP per week from regular play
  • Best value: Clothing and emotes over cars (cars return toAutoshow eventually)
  • Budget: Prioritize limited-time items that never return

Back-to-Back Seasons

If you miss a season, its rewards return through the Auction House or seasonal playlists at a later date. Rare items may become unobtainable for months or years.
