Season 1 Championships

Summer Thunder Championship

5-race series culminating in the Mt. Tsukuba sprint finale.

  • Event 1: Hakone Street Sprint — 3.2km urban course
  • Event 2: Fuji Freeway — 8km high-speed highway race
  • Event 3: Osaka Bay Circuit — 5km technical track
  • Event 4: Kyoto Countryside Rally — 12km mixed surface
  • Event 5: Mt. Tsukuba Sprint — 6km hill climb finale

Touge Invitational

Unlocked after completing the main championship. 3-leg touge race through the Izu Peninsula with S1-class cars.

Forzathon Points Strategy

Summer Thunder offers 8-10 Forzathon Points per day through daily challenges. The weekly total of 420+ FP can be earned through:

  • Daily Challenges: 3-5 FP each, simple tasks like "Win a Street Race"
  • Weekly Challenge: 50 FP for completing a specific multi-stage challenge
  • Seasonal Events: 15-25 FP for completing PR stunts at seasonal targets
  • Forzathon Arcade: 10-20 FP from arcade games on the map

Recommended spending: Prioritize the Summer Festival livery (300 FP, limited quantity) before cars appear in the Autoshow.
