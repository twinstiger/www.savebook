📅 Official Patch Update Timeline

Stay informed about every Forza Horizon 6 update. Each patch brings new features, bug fixes, balance changes and quality of life improvements. We track every update as it releases.

🔧 Version Update History

Version 1.200.0 — "Autumn Horizon" Update (October 2025)

  • New Map Region: Added Chugoku/Shikoku region — Hiroshima, Okayama, Matsuyama, Takamatsu. New city area and mountain routes.
  • New Vehicles: 25 new cars added including Toyota GR Supra RZ, Mazda RX-7 FC3S, Nissan S2000 AP1, Honda NSX NA1, Porsche 911 Turbo 930.
  • Photo Mode Improvements: New filters (Cinema, Vintage Japan, Sunset Drift), manual focus slider, composition grid overlay.
  • New Seasonal Event: Autumn Leaves Festival — exclusive rewards include Maple Leaf vinyl pack and Drift Camera effect.
  • Bug Fixes: Fixed collision on Hakone hairpin. Resolved snow accumulation bug on Gotemba route. Improved AI traffic spawning in Kyoto Gion area.
  • Balance Changes: AWD cars slightly nerfed in acceleration. RWD cars gain +5% grip on wet surfaces.
  • Festival Site Upgrade: New "Retro Arcade" festival building with mini-games and exclusive rewards.

Version 1.150.0 — "Summer Splash" Update (July 2025)

  • Okinawa Expansion: Added Okinawa mainland and 5 remote islands — Zamami, Geruma, Tokashiki, Ikei, and Tonaki. Total of 47 new roads.
  • New Barn Finds: 4 new barn locations in Okinawa region with exclusive 1970s Japanese classic cars.
  • Marine Sports: New boat physics with jet ski and sailing yacht vehicles. Marine seasonal championships.
  • New Vehicle Class: Introduced "Vintage Rally" category with 12 classic rally cars from 1970s-1990s.
  • Online Multiplayer: Expanded lobby size to 72 players. New "Convoy Race" mode for team-based competitions.
  • Photo Mode: New underwater camera mode for marine activities. Depth markers and marine life reference guide.
  • Controller Update: Added adaptive trigger resistance settings for Series X|S controllers.

Version 1.100.0 — "Winter Wonderland" Update (December 2024)

  • Hokkaido Snow Region: Full snow physics and winter road conditions. 200+ new roads in Tohoku and Hokkaido. Snow tire requirement for certain events.
  • Snow Vehicle Pack: 18 new snow-specific vehicles including rally sleds, snow plows, and winter edition SUVs.
  • New Festival Site: "Hokkaido Snow Festival" site with ice sculpture building feature and firework shows.
  • Barn Find Expansion: 3 new winter barn finds in remote Hokkaido locations. Requires winter conditions to access.
  • Seasonal Challenge Series: "Winter Racing Championship" — 8-week event series with exclusive rewards.
  • Weather System: Enhanced dynamic weather with snow accumulation. Snow melts after 48 hours of clear conditions in-game.
  • Photo Mode: Snow particle effects, frost overlay filter, aurora borealis effect in northern regions.

Version 1.050.0 — "Autumn Harvest" Update (October 2024)

  • Kansai Region Expansion: Added Osaka, Kyoto, Nara, Kobe, Himeji areas. 180+ new roads including mountain touge routes.
  • New Kyoto Barn Find: Hidden barn behind Fushimi Inari shrine with exclusive 1960s Toyota.
  • Festival Mode Enhancement: Added "Street Race Night" event chain in Osaka with neon lights and urban challenges.
  • New Vehicle Tuning: Race adjustable rear wing aerodynamics — now adjustable in 10-degree increments.
  • Photo Mode: New Kyoto-style filters (Nishiki market, temple gardens), lantern light sources.
  • Bug Fixes: Fixed Tokyo speed board detection at night. Corrected Kyoto Gion alley road width.

Version 1.020.0 — "Summer Festival" Update (July 2024)

  • First Major DLC: "Horizon Bonus Pack 1" — includes 50 new cars, new event lab blueprints, new clothing items.
  • Online Improvements: Reduced matchmaking time by 60%. Added cross-play between Xbox and PC.
  • Photo Mode: New sunset and night filters for Tokyo and Osaka areas. Time-lapse capture mode.
  • Event Lab: Added ability to share custom events and subscribe to featured community events.
  • Vehicle Fixes: Corrected brake bias on Toyota AE86. Fixed differential settings on Nissan S15.
  • Festival Site: New summer decorations — paper lanterns, festival stalls, water fountains.

Version 1.000.0 — "Launch Day" (April 3, 2026)

  • Game Release: Forza Horizon 6 launches on Xbox Series X|S, PC, Game Pass.
  • Initial Content: 500+ vehicles, full Japan map (Kanto, Chubu, Kansai regions), base game content.
  • Base Game Vehicles: Includes wide selection of modern and classic cars across all categories.
  • Initial Game Modes: Road Racing, Drift, Off-road, Drag Racing, Time Trial, Showcase Events.

🎉 Seasonal Events — Annual Event Calendar

Forza Horizon 6 runs a rotating calendar of seasonal events. Some events recur annually while others are one-time limited offerings. Complete events to earn exclusive rewards unavailable through any other means.

📅 Annual Event Calendar

February — Winter Festival & Abashiri Prison Event

  • Abashiri Prison Ice Race: Unique time-limited event on frozen lake near Abashiri Prison museum. Ice racing with studded tire requirements. Reward: Abashiri Prison Barn Find access.
  • Sapporo Snow Festival: Ice sculpture building at festival site. Player-submitted designs featured. Reward: Ice Sculpture commemorative item.
  • Valentine's Day Multiplayer Marathon: 48-hour multiplayer event with team point accumulation. Reward: Heart-Theme vehicle cosmetics.

March-April — Spring & Cherry Blossom Season

  • Hanami Drift Festival: Cherry blossom viewing touge events at Arashiyama and Mt. Fuji base. Reward: Sakura petal smoke effect and spring vehicle livery.
  • Horizon 6th Anniversary Event: Anniversary celebration with bonus credit rewards, returning limited vehicles for re-collection, and special showcase events. Reward: Anniversary trophy and exclusive emote.
  • Easter Egg Hunt: Hidden collectible hunt across Japan — find painted eggs at scenic locations. Reward: Bunny ears driver gear and Easter egg car horn.

May — Golden Week Japan Event

  • Golden Week Rally Challenge: Long-distance rally event covering full length of Japan. Reward: Rally King title and premium rally suit.
  • All-Japan Touge Championship: Elite touge racing event with qualifying rounds and finals. Reward: "Touge Legend" title and exclusive vehicle.
  • Retro Car Showcase: Special festival featuring all 1970s classic cars. Display your classics for credit rewards. Reward: Vintage parking permit cosmetic.

July-August — Summer Festival

  • Tanabata Star Festival: Summer night events with firework shows along river banks. Reward: Star pattern underglow and wish paper wall decorations.
  • Beach Party Week: Okinawa beach event with marine sports and sunset competitions. Reward: Beach umbrella car accessory and surfboard rack.
  • Summer Speed Challenge: Highest average speed challenge across all road types. Reward: Speed Demon title and aerodynamic body kit.
  • Obon Ancestor Racing: Traditional event honoring racing heritage. Reward: Ancestor spirits cosmetic effects and vintage racing colors.

September — Tsukimi Moon Viewing

  • Moon Viewing Rally: Night driving event visiting traditional moon viewing spots. Reward: Full moon headlight pattern and moon viewing lantern accessory.
  • Harvest Festival Road Trip: Country road event through rural Japan with farm visits. Reward: Harvest basket car accessory and farmer jacket driver gear.

October-November — Autumn Leaves Festival

  • Autumn Colors Photography Event: Photo competition at scenic autumn locations. Reward: Photography equipment cosmetic items.
  • Kansai Culture Festival: Osaka/Kyoto/Nara event with historical vehicle parade and shrine visits. Reward: Traditional Japanese racing colors livery.
  • Halloween Horror Drive: Night event with spooky themes — haunted locations, ghost vehicle decorations. Reward: Pumpkin headlight pattern and ghost car livery.
  • Autumn Leaves Drift Challenge: Mountain drift event with autumn scenery. Reward: Maple leaf smoke effect and autumn colors body kit.

December — Year End Celebration

  • Christmas New Year Carnival: Holiday festive event with lights, decorations, gift collection. Reward: Christmas wreath car accessory and holiday horn sounds.
  • 12 Days of FH6: Daily login bonuses and special mini-events December 1-12. Reward: 12 days of progressively rare items including exclusive vehicles.
  • Year End Showdown: Competitive event series with leaderboard prizes. Reward: Year champion title and champion banner cosmetic.

🏆 Community Challenges & Competitive Events

Forza Horizon 6 features ongoing community challenges where players compete globally for exclusive rewards, leaderboard recognition, and special titles.

🌍 Weekly Forza Monthly Challenge

  • Description: New challenge released every Tuesday requiring specific driving skills across Japan
  • Duration: 7 days to complete from release date
  • Reward: Exclusive car and cosmetic item unique to that month
  • Leaderboard: Top 10% receive bonus credit reward and special badge

🎯 Forzathon Daily Challenges

  • Description: Three new daily challenges every day covering diverse driving scenarios
  • Example Challenges: "Break 5 speed boards in Hakone", "Complete a drift skill chain of 50,000 points", "Photograph a landmark at sunset"
  • Forzathon Points: Earn points redeemable for exclusive cars and items in Forzathon Shop

🏁 Horizon Open Drift Championship

  • Format: 8-week championship series in multiplayer drift events
  • Scoring: Cumulative points across weekly events
  • Rewards: Drift King livery pack, Drift Star car customization, "Drift Legend" title
  • Season: New championship starts every 8 weeks

📸 Photo Challenge Series

  • Monthly Photo Contest: Submit photos to Forza community gallery. Top-voted photos featured in official media.
  • Theme Challenges: Weekly photo themes like "Tokyo Nights", "Mountain Dawn", "Beach Sunset"
  • Reward: Camera icon cosmetic, special photographer titles, featured photo frame

🚗 Car Photography World Tour

  • Description: Visit 50 designated Photo Spots across Japan and capture the assigned vehicle type
  • Duration: Rolling 90-day event
  • Rewards: Photo Spot collection badge, professional photographer title, exclusive camera items

📋 Community Seasonal Championship

  • Spring Championship: Team-based mountain touge competition. Team captains organize roster of 8 drivers.
  • Summer Championship: Beach racing and marine event series. Points accumulated across all event types.
  • Autumn Championship: Mixed surface rally across Kansai region. Dirt, asphalt, and snow stages.
  • Winter Championship: Ice racing and snow drift competition in Hokkaido. Studded tire mandatory.
  • Overall Season Winner: 4-region championship winners compete in Year-End Grand Prix for championship trophy and exclusive hypercar.

🛠️ Community Creations — Event Lab Spotlight

  • Featured Events: Community-created events featured by Turn 10 developers each month
  • Creator Recognition: Featured creators receive Forza Horizon merchandise and in-game credit rewards
  • Blueprint Competition: Quarterly competition for best Event Lab track design. Winner gets their track in the official festival rotation.
