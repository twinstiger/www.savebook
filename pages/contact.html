Response Time

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please include "URGENT" in your email subject line.

💡 Tips for Faster Response

  • Use a clear and descriptive subject line
  • Include specific page URLs when reporting issues
  • Provide screenshots if applicable
  • Be concise but include all relevant details

Frequently Asked Questions

A: Yes! We welcome community-submitted tuning setups. Please send them to feedback@savebook.net with detailed descriptions and test results.

A: We update guides regularly based on game patches, seasonal events, and community feedback. Major updates are typically released within 1-2 weeks of game changes.

Q: Do you accept advertising partnerships?
