All Hidden Barn Finds Full Location Guide

Barn Finds are special hidden classic cars scattered across the Japan map. These rare vehicles cannot be purchased directly and must be discovered through exploration:

  • Mt. Fuji Base Barn Find: Hidden in forest area near mountain base, unlocks 1970s classic JDM sports car
  • Tokyo Bay Warehouse: Located in industrial port district, contains rare imported muscle car
  • Kyoto Temple Hills: Found behind ancient shrine, rewards vintage Japanese luxury sedan
  • Hokkaido Snow Farm: Buried in rural farmland, reveals classic rally championship car
  • Okinawa Coastal Cave: Hidden in seaside cliff cave, unlocks exclusive beach edition convertible

Car Performance Upgrade Guide

Understanding upgrade priorities helps maximize your vehicle potential:

  • Engine Swaps: Increase horsepower but may affect weight balance
  • Tire Compounds: Choose between grip, durability and weather suitability
  • Suspension Geometry: Adjust camber, toe and caster for track-specific handling
  • Aerodynamic Kits: Improve downforce at cost of reduced top speed
  • Weight Reduction: Remove unnecessary components for better power-to-weight ratio
