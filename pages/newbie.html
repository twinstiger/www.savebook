📈 Progression System — Level Faster & Unlock More ▶

Forza Horizon 6 has three parallel progression tracks: Driver Level, Car Mastery, and Festival Site expansion. Understanding how they work helps you prioritize activities.

🏆 Driver Level — Your Overall Rank

  • Driver Level increases by earning XP from all activities: racing, exploring, drifting, speed runs.
  • Every level up awards a Wheelspin — contains credits, clothing, car horns, or rare vehicles.
  • Driver Level 50 is the current cap — beyond level 50, overflow XP converts to bonus credits (500 XP per 1,000 CR).
  • Higher Driver Levels unlock new event types and difficulty tiers in Festival Playlist.

🚗 Car Mastery — Individual Car Progression

  • Each car has its own mastery tree — earn XP per car to unlock perks.
  • Mastery perks include: credit bonuses for using the car, discount on parts, bonus skill points, exclusive paint colors.
  • Focus mastery on your "main" car to maximize credit return — an 80% mastery bonus adds up.
  • Check Car Mastery from the garage — some perks are hidden and require specific driving to unlock.

🎪 Festival Site Expansion

  • Main festival sites upgrade in stages — each upgrade unlocks more car storage slots and new event types.
  • Gas Station purchases at festival sites unlock: additional garage space, exclusive brands, performance upgrades.
  • Priority: Buy enough garage slots to store 5-10 cars minimum. More storage = more variety without selling.

🛣️ Road Types & How to Drive Them

  • Expressway / Highway: Wide lanes, gentle curves. Top speed territory — use for speed skill chains. Watch for merging traffic at interchanges.
  • National Road: 2-lane roads connecting cities. Mix of gentle and sharp curves. Good for skill chains and exploration. Watch for trucks.
  • Prefectural Road: Narrower winding roads. Common in rural areas. The bread-and-butter of touge driving. Watch for oncoming traffic.
  • Mountain Pass (Touge): Very narrow, steep gradient, tight hairpins. Pure touge territory. Drive defensively — there's no run-off area.
  • City Streets: Dense intersections, traffic lights, pedestrians. Use lower speeds and anticipate stops. Night runs have neon reflections and lower traffic.
  • Dirt / Gravel: Off-road tracks and mountain forest roads. Lower grip — modulate throttle carefully. AWD and off-road tires essential in snow.

🚗 Driving Fundamentals — Core Skills for All Players ▶

These fundamental techniques apply to every racing discipline in FH6 — road racing, touge, drift, time trial, and off-road. Master these basics before moving to advanced techniques.

🛞 Steering Techniques

  • Targeted Apprxach: Look where you want to go, not at the obstacle. Your hands follow your eyes — look at the apex, not the wall.
  • Hand Position: Use "10 and 2" or "quarter-to" grip for smooth inputs. Avoid sawing the wheel back and forth — smooth steering = smooth car.
  • Weight Transfer: Ease off throttle before turning to transfer weight to front tires, then add throttle to rotate. Called "trail braking" — essential for touge.
  • Opposite Lock: When the rear steps out, turn the wheel into the direction of the slide (same as spin). Countersteer maintains angle while allowing throttle control.

🛑 Braking Zones — The Key to Fast Lap Times

  • Early braking zones (90°+ corners): Brake 50-80 meters before corner entry, release brake before turn-in. Reduces speed to 40-60% for safe apex speed.
  • Medium corners (30-90°): Brake 30-50 meters out, maintain light braking through initial turn-in, release at apex for progressive corner exit.
  • Fast sweepers (under 30°): Lift off throttle briefly (no brake), car rotates naturally at high speed. Add very light brake if needed for stability.
  • Hairpin technique: Heavy brake at 100m marker, downshift to 2nd or 3rd gear, turn in late, then power out from the apex — most critical skill for touge.

🎮 Throttle Control

  • Progressive throttle: Never floor it instantly from low speed — smoothly apply throttle to prevent wheelspin and maintain traction.
  • Mid-corner throttle: Adding throttle mid-corner rotates the car (oversteer) while lifting rotates less (understeer). Use throttle as a steering tool.
  • Exit throttle: The most important moment — apply full throttle the instant the car points toward track exit. Earlier throttle = faster lap.
  • Hill climbing: Reduce throttle on uphill to prevent front wheelspin, increase slightly on downhill to maintain grip.

🅰️ Handbrake Usage

  • Drift initiation: Tap handbrake while turning hard to lock rear wheels and start a drift. Works from 30-80 km/h depending on car weight and grip.
  • Scandinavian flick: Sweep the car opposite to intended direction, then rapidly turn into the drift. No handbrake needed — more advanced technique.
  • Emergency braking: Used in rally — handbrake with brake to lock rear wheels while keeping front grip for steering.
  • Weight transfer: Handbrake while turning also transfers weight to front, improving turn-in on AWD cars during slow corners.

🔄 Clutch Shifting (Manual Transmission)

  • Upshift: Reduce throttle slightly, press clutch, lift gear, release clutch, reapply throttle. Clutch press should be quick — hold too long causes engine braking.
  • Downshift: Match RPM to avoid "bang" synchromesh wear: press clutch, shift to lower gear, blip throttle (short press), release clutch. "Heel-toe" technique synchronizes engine RPM with wheel speed.
  • Optimal shift points: For most cars, shift at 6000-7000 RPM for maximum power. Turbo cars shift at 6500-7500 RPM. Consult individual car specs for redline.
  • Engine braking: In long downhill sections, shift to lower gear and let engine resistance slow the car — this preserves brakes and provides stability.

📳 Vibration & Feedback Tuning

  • Steering Vibration (0-100%): Controller wheel vibration on rumble strips and rough surfaces. Set to 30-50% for road racing, 0-20% for drift (avoids distracting vibration during slides).
  • Collision Vibration (0-100%): Vibration on wall and car contact. Set to 100% for maximum impact awareness. Reduce to 50% if causing fatigue during long sessions.
  • Engine Vibration (0-100%): Vibration intensity matching RPM. Aesthetics — does not affect gameplay.
  • Trigger Resistance (Series X|S): Adaptive trigger brake resistance. Set to "Sport" mode for precise brake pressure, "Off" for lighter feel.
