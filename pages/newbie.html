Quick Start

🎮Controller Settings

Recommended: Acceleration/brake sensitivity at 7, steering at 5 for beginners. Turn off traction assist for better control.

Controller

⌨️Keyboard Settings

W/A/S/D for steering, Space for handbrake, E for gear up, Q for gear down. Consider rebinding handbrake to Left Shift.

Keyboard

🖥️Display Settings

Field of View 90-100 for better peripheral vision. Camera height +2 from default for clearer road view.

Display

📳Vibration & Feedback

Medium vibration feedback recommended. Strong force feedback can make precise control harder on long sessions.

Feel
  • Start with Default assist settings — turn off one at a time as you improve
  • ABS on is fine for beginners, turn off as you learn threshold braking
  • Stability control at 1-2 for RWD cars, 0 for AWD drift builds
  • Camera shake off for cleaner recording

🛞Steering Technique

Start turning before the corner, not during. Weight transfer is your friend — brake in a straight line, turn after.

Corner Entry

🛞Throttle Control

Trail braking (light brake while turning in) extends grip. On exit, add throttle gradually as you straighten the wheel.

Throttle

🏔️Mountain Roads

Slow in, fast out. The mountain rewards patience — entry speed is everything. 400-500hp RWD is the ideal learning tool.

TouGE

🏙️City Racing

Avoid hard braking before 90-degree corners. Stiffer front suspension reduces nose dive. Choose high turn-in response cars.

City
  • Look where you want to go — your car follows your eyes
  • Brake BEFORE the corner, not during — weight transfer kills rotation
  • Use both brakes and throttle together for heel-toe downshifts
  • Learn to feel when the rear tires start to slide — that's your grip limit

💰Credits Economy

Complete Festival Playlist for ~₵200,000/hour. Auction House flips for rare cars. Wheelspins from Seasonal rewards.

Credits

🎁Forzathon Points

3-5 Forzathon Points per daily challenge. 50+ FP per week from regular play. Prioritize limited-time items in Forzathon Shop.

FP

🏆Seasonal Rewards

Complete Summer Championship for exclusive cars. Seasonal rewards are free — no extra cost beyond event completion.

Seasonal

🚗Car Selection

Start with 3 cars: one for circuit (A-class), one for touge (A-class RWD), one for speed (S1 AWD). Expand collection as credits grow.

Starter
  • Prioritize Barn Finds over Autoshow purchases — they're free and often the best cars
  • Don't upgrade D-class cars past B-class — sell and buy the next tier instead
  • Festival Playlist completion is the most reliable credit income
  • Save Forzathon Points for exclusive clothing — cars eventually return to Autoshow
