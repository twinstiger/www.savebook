What Are Barn Finds?

Barn finds are hidden classic and rare vehicles scattered across the Japan map in Forza Horizon 6. These special cars cannot be purchased from dealerships and must be discovered through exploration. Once found, they require restoration before use.

How Barn Finds Work

  • Listen for radio hints about abandoned vehicles in specific regions
  • Explore remote areas, old farms, and abandoned buildings
  • Look for glowing markers or distinctive barn structures
  • Discover the car and send it to your garage for restoration
  • Wait for restoration completion (varies by car rarity)

Barn Find Tips & Tricks

Efficient Search Strategies

  • Use Fast Travel: Unlock all fast travel points to quickly reach different regions
  • Follow Radio Clues: Horizon Radio provides vague location hints - listen carefully
  • Explore Off-Road: Most barn finds are away from main roads and cities
  • Check Elevated Areas: Many are located on hills or mountains with scenic views
  • Look for Structures: Old barns, warehouses, and abandoned buildings are common hiding spots

Restoration Tips

  • Restoration time varies from a few minutes to several hours
  • Rarer cars take longer to restore
  • You can continue playing while waiting for restoration
  • Some cars may require special parts for full restoration
