Seasonal Weather Racing Guide

FH6 adds dynamic seasonal weather systems including rain, snow and cherry blossom seasons, which completely change vehicle handling and track friction. Master weather racing rules is essential for stable victories.

Rain Racing Strategy

  • Adjust traction control to 80% to avoid slipping on wet asphalt
  • Must equip professional wet tires for all rainy street events
  • Brake 20% earlier than usual to prevent overshooting corners

Snow Racing Strategy

  • Switch to dedicated snow tires immediately when entering snow zones
  • Reduce overall speed by 30% on mountain snowy passes
  • Avoid sudden steering and throttle inputs to prevent spinning out

City Street Racing Strategy

Tokyo urban street racing scene with complex continuous corners and short straights

Urban tracks in Tokyo and Japanese downtown areas feature dense continuous corners, short straights and complex road turns. The core of city racing is stable cornering and reasonable speed control.

City Racing Core Skills

  • Brake early before continuous tight corners to avoid understeer
  • Apex pointing use wide outer tracks to cut inner corners for faster exit speed
  • Choose balanced handling cars instead of extreme high-speed supercars
  • Avoid wall collisions which greatly reduce average speed

Tokyo District Key Corners

  • Shibuya Crossing: Wide intersection - full throttle after pedestrians clear
  • Ginza Straight: Longest straight - use drafting for top speed
  • Akihabara Backstreets: Tight 90-degree corners - brake to 30km/h
  • Rainy Tokyo: Reduced grip - increase following distance by 50%

Best City Racing Cars

  • Honda Civic Type R: Ultimate hot hatch, perfect for tight corners
  • Toyota GR Yaris: AWD compact monster, instant acceleration
  • Nissan Z: Good balance of power and handling
  • BMW M3/M4: Premium choice for experienced drivers

Mountain Touge Drift Track Tips

Classic Japanese mountain touge drifting track, the core gameplay of FH6

Mountain touge tracks are the most popular feature in FH6. These winding uphill and downhill roads are designed for drifting challenges and require precise throttle and steering control.

TouGE Drift Core Techniques

  • Initiation: Speed up to 50-70km/h then pull handbrake while turning hard to start the drift
  • Counter-steering: Turn steering wheel opposite to spin direction to maintain angle
  • Throttle control: Gentle acceleration during drift to control rear slide speed
  • Clutch kicking: Short tap on clutch during drift to add angle without gaining too much speed

Uphill vs Downhill Racing

  • Uphill touge: momentum is key - use initial speed and let car climb while drifting
  • Downhill touge: engine braking helps control speed - shift to lower gears
  • Use lightweight 400-500hp cars for best power-to-weight ratio

Best Touge Drift Cars

  • Nissan 370Z: Perfect entry drift car, balanced handling
  • Toyota AE86/Sprinter Trueno: Legendary touge car, great for technical drifts
  • Mazda RX-7: Rotary power, excellent for continuous drifts
  • Honda S2000: High-rev power, for advanced players

Time Trial Fast Lap Strategy

Time trial events require no collision, perfect cornering and maximum exit speed. Players can use our professional track tuning setups to break personal best lap times.

Time Trial Pro Tips

  • Perfection above speed - Clean laps beat fast but reckless laps
  • Late braking - Brake later into corners than normal racing
  • Trail braking - Hold brake while turning to rotate car
  • Early throttle - Apply gas as soon as car points to track exit
  • No collision penalty - Touch walls = 2 seconds added per hit

Corner Entry Formula

  • Tight hairpin: Brake hard → Turn → Maintain low gear → Power out
  • Fast sweeper: Lift throttle → Minor brake → Full throttle through
  • Chicane: Brake once → Double apEX for both corners
  • Hairpin + straight: Short brake → Max rotation → Instant acceleration

Recommended Time Trial Cars

  • Porsche 911 GT3 RS: Ultimate track weapon
  • Lamborghini Revuelto: Hybrid supercar power
  • Ferrari 296 GTB: Balanced extreme performance
  • Honda NSX Type S: Best value for consistent times
