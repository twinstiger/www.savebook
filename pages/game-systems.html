FH6 Game Systems Guide

Progression, Car Passes, Skills & New Features Explained

Three Core Progression Systems

🎮 Driver Level

Gain XP by completing races, discovering new locations, and performing skills. Higher levels unlock new events, cars and customization options.

  • Earn XP from all activities
  • Unlock festival sites at key levels
  • Access higher difficulty events

🌟 Skill Trees

Spend skill points earned from driving to unlock permanent bonuses and abilities across multiple categories.

  • Racing skills: Better handling and speed
  • Exploration skills: Map reveals and fast travel
  • Collection skills: Bonus rewards and credits

🏆 Reputation System

Build reputation with different racing disciplines to unlock specialized events and exclusive rewards.

  • Street Racing reputation
  • Drift Zone reputation
  • Off-Road reputation
  • High Speed reputation

Car Pass System

The Car Pass is a premium content system that provides regular vehicle deliveries throughout the year:

How Car Pass Works

  • Monthly Deliveries: Receive 2-4 new cars each month directly to your garage
  • Exclusive Vehicles: Access cars not available in regular shops or barn finds
  • Variety: Mix of classic, modern, race and special edition vehicles
  • Instant Ownership: No need to win or purchase - cars arrive automatically

Car Pass Benefits

  • Guaranteed access to premium vehicles
  • Early access to some cars before public release
  • Special liveries and customization options
  • Better value than purchasing individual cars
  • Includes both performance cars and collector items

Note: Car Pass is typically included in Deluxe and Premium editions, or available as separate DLC purchase.

New Gameplay Features in FH6

Forza Horizon 6 introduces several innovative features that enhance the racing experience

🗾 Japan Map Diversity

Experience contrasting environments from neon-lit Tokyo streets to serene mountain touge passes, coastal highways and rural countryside.

🌦️ Dynamic Weather

Seasonal weather changes affect driving conditions. Rain reduces grip, snow requires winter tires, and cherry blossom season offers unique visual experiences.

🏔️ Touge Drifting

New mountain pass racing format emphasizing drift skills, precise cornering and maintaining momentum through tight switchbacks.

🚗 Enhanced Customization

Deeper tuning options, expanded body kits, custom liveries and performance parts for complete vehicle personalization.

📱 Connected Features

Seamless integration with Forza community, shared designs, online competitions and seasonal championships.

🎯 Skill Challenges

Diverse challenge types including speed traps, drift zones, danger signs and street scene events scattered across the map.

Maximizing Your Progression

Efficient Leveling Tips

  • Complete Story Missions: Highest XP rewards and unlock essential features
  • Chain Skills: Perform consecutive skills without crashing for bonus XP
  • Explore Thoroughly: Discovering roads and locations grants substantial XP
  • Seasonal Events: Limited-time events offer double XP and exclusive rewards
  • Photo Mode: Taking photos of cars and scenery provides small but easy XP

Smart Skill Point Allocation

  • Prioritize exploration skills early for faster map completion
  • Invest in racing skills matching your preferred event types
  • Save some points for seasonal bonus skills
  • Don't spread too thin - focus on 2-3 skill trees initially
