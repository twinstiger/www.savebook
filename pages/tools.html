📷 Photo Mode — Full Feature Guide

Photo Mode in FH6 is one of the most powerful and versatile in any racing game. Access it by pressing View/Gallery button while your car is stationary or in free roam. Every setting affects the final image — understand each to capture professional-quality screenshots.

🎨 Photo Mode Settings — Complete Breakdown

📐 Lens Settings

  • Focal Length (mm): Controls field of view. 24mm = wide cinematic. 85mm = portrait compression. 200mm = dramatic telephoto. Range: 10-400mm.
  • Aperture (f-stop): Controls depth of field blur intensity. f/1.8 = very blurred background. f/16 = everything in focus. Lower f-stop = more blur + more light.
  • Focus Distance (m): Manual focus slider. Use this instead of auto-focus to set exactly where sharpness begins. 0 = infinity.
  • Depth of Field Intensity: Master dial for background blur amount. 0 = off (everything sharp). 100 = maximum cinematic blur.

🌈 Color & Lighting Settings

  • Exposure: -3 to +3 stops. Controls image brightness. Start at 0 and adjust based on time of day and environment brightness.
  • Contrast: -50 to +50. Higher = punchier colors and deeper blacks. Lower = desaturated, muted look.
  • Highlights: -100 to +100. Controls brightness of bright areas (sky, headlights, sun reflections). Reduce to recover detail in bright zones.
  • Shadows: -100 to +100. Lifts or crushes dark areas. Increase for detail in undercarriage, increase shadows for moody look.
  • Saturation: -100 to +100. Self-explanatory. Zero = black and white, 50 = natural vibrant, 100 = hyper saturated.
  • White Balance: -100 to +100. Negative (blue) = warmer day, Positive (yellow) = cooler night. Manual control overrides automatic.
  • Vignette: 0-100. Adds dark edges to frame, drawing attention to center. 15-25 is subtle and effective. Higher creates dramatic tunnel effect.

🌅 Time & Atmosphere Controls

  • Time of Day: Slider from dawn to midnight. Full day cycle simulation. Sunrise and sunset provide best lighting. Midnight provides dark moody shots.
  • Weather: Clear, partly cloudy, overcast, rain, snow. Weather changes affect lighting and surface grip. Rain creates reflections and wet surface look.
  • Season Override: Force spring/summer/autumn/winter regardless of actual season. Useful for specific photo types year-round.

🎬 Composition & Advanced Features

  • Rule of Thirds Grid: Turn on to align subject at intersection points — creates balanced, professional composition.
  • Diagonal Lines Grid: For architectural and dynamic shots — aligns with perspective lines.
  • Golden Ratio Grid: Spiral overlay for organic, naturally balanced composition.
  • Focus Tracking: When enabled, camera follows car if it moves — useful for action photos.
  • Lens Flare: Natural or dramatic flare effects from bright light sources. Subtle at 10-20%, heavy at 80-100%.
  • Motion Blur: Adds streaking to moving parts — creates sense of speed. Use with care on stationary shots.

📤 Export Settings

  • 4K Resolution: Highest quality export at 3840x2160. Use for prints, 4K displays, detailed zoom shots.
  • 1080p Full HD: Standard quality, smaller file size. Suitable for social media and general use.
  • Share to Photo Mode Gallery: Saved to in-game photo library for sharing with community.
  • Console Screenshot: Xbox/PC native screenshot captures at system resolution — often higher than in-game camera resolution.

🏁 Event Lab — Create & Customize Events

Event Lab allows you to create completely custom racing events using the FH6 road network. Design your own circuits, drift zones, and challenges, then share them with the community and subscribe to community creations.

📐 Event Creation Step by Step

  1. Open Event Lab: Access from main menu > Create > Event Lab. Select event type (Circuit, Sprint, Drift, etc.)
  2. Select Route: Drive or teleport to your starting point. Press A to set start. Drive your intended route. Press A at checkpoints and finish line.
  3. Place Checkpoints: Add checkpoints along your route to prevent shortcutting. Minimum 2 checkpoints required for circuits.
  4. Set Rules: Choose weather, time of day, car restrictions, lap count, traffic settings, AI difficulty.
  5. Test Run: Play your event before publishing — identify broken checkpoints or impossible turns.
  6. Publish: Name your event, add description, select tags, and publish to community. Turn 10 reviews submissions before public listing.

🔧 Event Settings & Options

  • Car Restrictions: Choose allowed manufacturers, car types, PI class limits, or open class (any car).
  • Weather Override: Set specific weather for your event or use game world current weather.
  • Traffic Toggle: Enable/disable traffic during your event. Traffic adds challenge but can break tight circuits.
  • AI Difficulty: Set AI competitors from 1-10. 1 = very slow, 10 = expert-level times.
  • Lap Count: 1 to 50 laps. Circuit races typically use 3-10 laps depending on circuit length.
  • Countdown Time: 3, 5, or 10 second race start countdown. Drift events use 5 seconds to allow preparation.
  • Respawn Behavior: None (hardcore), on road only, or always (arcade). Choose based on event seriousness.

🏆 Event Lab Featured & Featured Creators

  • Turn 10 selects community events for "Featured" status — these appear on the Horizon Festival menu.
  • Featured events receive high traffic and can earn creator recognition and in-game rewards.
  • Submit your event 2 weeks before major seasonal events for best chance of review.
  • Keep event descriptions clear: mention difficulty, car restrictions, and estimated completion time.

🎯 Blueprint Design Best Practices

  • Start simple: Begin with an oval or rectangle. Add complexity once basic flow works.
  • Maintain flow: Each piece should lead naturally to the next. Avoid 90° turns that break momentum unless intentional.
  • Consider car class: High-speed circuits need long straights. Technical circuits need tight corners with good visibility.
  • Test with different cars: What works for a slow Civic may be impossible for a GT-R.
  • Use checkpoints generously: Checkpoints prevent shortcutting and allow player respawn at correct position.
  • Mind the gaps: Ensure pieces connect seamlessly — watch for gaps between road segments.

🔧 Blueprint Settings

  • Track Length: Total circuit distance from start to finish
  • Elevation Change: Total height variation — flat, rolling, mountainous
  • Turn Count: Number of turns — classified as simple (under 8), technical (8-15), or complex (15+)
  • Difficulty Rating: Auto-calculated based on turn sharpness and length — affects matchmaking
  • Surface Type: Asphalt, dirt, mixed — affects tire selection and handling

🎪 Festival Site Customization

The Festival Site is your main hub in FH6. You can customize and upgrade festival sites to unlock more features, storage, and visual flair. Each region has at least one main festival site plus smaller outposts.

🎨 Customization Options

  • Festival Decoration Theme: Choose visual theme (Default, Summer, Winter, Festival, Anniversary) — changes flags, banners, lighting colors.
  • Car Display Stands: Showcase up to 5 personal cars on elevated platforms at your main festival site.
  • Billboard Messages: Set a custom message on your festival billboard — visible to other players in online sessions.
  • Music Volume: Adjust the festival's background music volume independently from game audio.
  • Player Emblem Display: Choose where your player crest appears — on flags, banners, and your character outfit.

🏆 Festival Site Ranks

  • Stage 1 (Default): Basic facilities, limited car slots, minimal customization.
  • Stage 2: Unlocks Gas Station + 10 additional car slots. Cost: 200,000 CR
  • Stage 3: Unlocks Clothing Shop + 10 additional slots. Cost: 400,000 CR
  • Stage 4: Unlocks Photo Studio + 10 additional slots. Cost: 600,000 CR
  • Stage 5 (Max): Unlocks Arcade + 20 additional slots. Cost: 1,000,000 CR

💡 Festival Site Tips

  • Prioritize Gas Station upgrades early — advanced tuning parts are essential for competitive racing.
  • Garage capacity matters — running out of car slots forces you to sell vehicles.
  • Spread festival sites across regions — buying small outposts in each region gives you fast travel options everywhere.
  • Photo Studio at festival site has different lighting setups than Photo Mode on the road — use both.

🎯 Skill Point Planner — Optimize Your Progression

Skill points in FH6 are earned through driving actions and must be allocated to the right skills for your play style. The Skill Planner helps you plan which skills to prioritize for maximum efficiency.

🔥 Skill Combo Multiplier System

  • Base Duration: Skill points in each category decay over 15 seconds of no activity in that category.
  • Combo Chain: Maintain activity in multiple skill categories simultaneously to build multiplier. Speed + Style = Drift Chain. Speed + Danger = Close Racing. All four = Perfect Score.
  • Multiplier Levels: 2x → 5x → 10x → 15x → 20x. Higher multiplier = exponentially more skill points earned per action.
  • Break vs. Fade: Taking big damage (wall collision) breaks the combo completely. Taking no damage allows combo to slowly fade — you can rebuild faster from faded state.

💰 Skill Points → Credits Conversion

  • Skill points accumulate in each category. When a category hits Level 50 (cap), overflow converts to bonus credits at 500 skill points per 1,000 CR.
  • At maximum efficiency, a 2-hour session with active skill chains can yield 5-8 million CR from skill overflow conversion alone.
  • Focus on maintaining 15x+ multiplier during skill farming runs to maximize the return per hour.
  • Use the "Skill Reward Bonus" car mastery perk — some cars give +10-20% extra skill conversion, significantly boosting CR income.
